About the Company

Family-Owned.
Mission-Focused.

Founded in 1994 by Orlando Teixeira, Expedite Precision Works is a family-owned CNC machine shop that has earned the confidence of engineers across the world's most demanding industries over three decades.

We manufacture prototype, low-volume, and high-volume close-tolerance parts in both metal and plastic — bringing state-of-the-art solutions to every job, from one-off development parts to sustained production runs requiring absolute consistency.

Our reputation rests on a single principle: if it can be machined, we find a way to make it right, on time.

What We Do

Core
Capabilities

State-of-the-art CNC machining across the full production spectrum — first-article prototypes through sustained high-volume runs, in virtually any machinable metal or engineering plastic.

01
CNC Milling

Multi-axis milling of complex geometries in aluminum, stainless, titanium, and engineering plastics. High-tolerance surface finishes on every run.

02
CNC Turning

Precision turning to tight diametral tolerances. Live tooling for compound features in a single setup — fewer operations, better accuracy.

03
Prototyping

First-article and development prototypes with full dimensional reports. Fast turnaround for design-iteration cycles on demanding programs.

04
High-Volume Production

Sustained production runs with full process controls and traceability. Consistent quality across large batches for certified, mission-critical supply chains.

05
Close-Tolerance Work

Specialty in challenging, close-tolerance components other shops decline. Proven on aerospace, medical, and semiconductor parts with stringent requirements.

06
Plastics & Metals

Full material range: aluminum alloys, stainless, titanium, Inconel, brass, PEEK, Delrin, PTFE, polycarbonate, and custom alloy specifications.
